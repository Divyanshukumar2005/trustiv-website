About Trustiv

Your Trusted Partner for Recruitment & HR Operations

We started Trustiv with one goal—to help businesses simplify recruitment and HR operations. By combining expert hiring solutions with reliable HR support, we enable organizations to build stronger teams, improve efficiency, and focus on sustainable growth.

Our approach

Not a vendor. A second desk in your operation.

"Our goal is simple — handle the people side of business, so founders can focus on growth."

Every engagement starts with understanding how you already work — your tools, your calendar, your standards for a "good hire." We fit into that, rather than asking you to fit into ours.

— 01

We work inside your process, not around it

Your ATS, your spreadsheets, your Slack — we show up where your team already works.

— 02

Every task is logged, not left to memory

Candidate stages, onboarding checklists, and attendance records are tracked and shared, so nothing depends on one person remembering.

— 03

We scale with your hiring calendar

A single open role or ten roles a quarter — the support model flexes with what you actually need this month.

— 04

Founders stay in the loop, not in the weeds

You see shortlists and decisions that matter. We handle the coordination that doesn't need to reach your desk.
